Common questions

What's included each week?

Mowing, string trimming around edges, and blowing clippings off your driveway and walkways. Landscaping, weeding and shrub trimming aren't included.

When will my first mow happen?

You choose a start date at least 72 hours out. We mow within a 1–3 day window of that date, based on our route.

Do I need to be home?

No. Leave the gate unlocked and pets inside on mow day. You can add a gate code or notes when you sign up.

What if it rains?

Rain can shift your mow within the 1–3 day service window. If it has to move further, we'll text you.

How do I pay?

After you sign up we send a secure Stripe link. Your card is billed weekly, plus 8.25% sales tax. No cash or checks to hand over.

How do I cancel?

Text us or email us with your name and address. We cancel the same day. There's no contract and no cancellation fee.

My grass is really overgrown. Is that a problem?

Not at all. If it's over 9 inches tall on the first visit, a one-time overgrowth fee of 50–100% of the weekly rate may apply. We'll always tell you before charging it.

Referral questions

When do credits get applied?

We review referrals the first week of each month and apply credits as a billing adjustment on your next Stripe bill.

Why is there a $600 yearly cap?

The IRS requires a 1099 for payments to an individual over $600 a year. To keep it simple during launch, referral earnings are capped there.

Do I need to be a customer?

No, anyone can refer. You don't need to be a lawn customer yourself.
